# Revenue sharing (project policy)

This document is a **project policy**, not a term of the
[GNU Affero General Public License](LICENSE). The AGPL itself does not require
revenue sharing. Nothing here changes your rights under the AGPL.

## The pledge

**[X]%** of **net commercial license revenue** — money received under
commercial licenses described in [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md),
after payment-processor fees, refunds, chargebacks, and taxes the project must
withhold or pay — is paid into a public community fund.

The fund is held by **[FISCAL HOST, e.g. Open Collective]** and is visible to
the public. Distributions go to maintainers and contributors of GleanWise.

`[X]` and `[FISCAL HOST, e.g. Open Collective]` are placeholders until the
project publishes final numbers and names a host. Until then, treat this file as
intent, not an operational commitment.

## Scope

- Applies only to revenue from **commercial licenses** of this codebase.
- Does not cover donations, sponsorships, or consulting unless those are later
  folded into the same fund by a separate decision.
- Does not create an employment, partnership, or fiduciary relationship with
  contributors beyond what the fiscal host’s terms provide.
